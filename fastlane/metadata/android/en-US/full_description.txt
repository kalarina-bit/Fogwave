Fogwave is a minimal, ad-free internet radio player for Lithuanian stations.

<b>Stations</b>
ZIP FM, Power Hit Radio, Power Hit Radio Gold, Rock FM, Relax FM, Lietus, M-1, M-1 Plius, Gold FM and RC.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Background playback with notification, lock-screen, headset and Bluetooth controls</li>
<li>Android Auto support</li>
<li>Automatic reconnection when the stream drops or the network comes back</li>
<li>Equalizer with presets and per-band control, loudness enhancer and dynamics processing</li>
<li>Favorites, recently played and similar stations</li>
<li>Four layouts — list, table, tiles and icons — with adjustable size</li>
<li>English, Russian, German, Lithuanian, Japanese and Chinese</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Audio is streamed directly from each broadcaster; Fogwave does not host or redistribute it.

Station names and logos belong to their respective owners.
